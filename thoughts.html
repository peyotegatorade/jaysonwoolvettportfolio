Thoughts on Animating.

Over the years, like many of the animators I've had the pleasure to meet, I've formulated some opinions on how to animate FX. Generally there are a few commonly recommended texts on the topic, but I find that they are really focused on animating in the Feature Film context. There's nothing wrong with that (and strongly recommend reading Elemental Magic and Essential Effects) and all the principles still apply, but the vast majority of us working will find themselves in TV studios. Some of these thoughts apply to us in these situations. Another thing I've found is that there are a lot of Harmony Tech tips that seem to be hidden knowledge.

Hopefully some of these can help you, like they have helped me.

If you enjoy these, please write me and let me know!
