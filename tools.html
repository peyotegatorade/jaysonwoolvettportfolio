Why?

I've never really been a big fan of digital tools, so for the most part, except for my animation, all the artwork I create is analogue. This has led me to spend an embarrassingly large amount of my life looking for my "the best" both in real life & online, which leads me to my next point.

Serendipity is the feeling of finding what you need when you aren't looking for it. While this list might point you to a path already well travelled, I encourage everyone to think for themselves, to keep an open mind & an open heart and to live a life worth expressing. Trust me, you'll find what your looking for in the most unexpected places, both in terms of instruments & inspiration.

Anyone who knows me, will know that I hold a special fondness in my heart for gift shops. Sure, I get it, they're usually nothing more than tchotchke kiosks, but they're also run by people whose whole life revolves around creating memories for us to take home, to put on a shelf and reminisce with our loved ones over a bottle of ice cold white wine. Our lives are miniature museums of memories, and one day, we all must exit thru the gift shop.

I'm also always interested in trying new things, so if you feel so inclined to write me about some of your favourites or if your a fan of any of these, I'd love to hear from you. I always love when fellow folk on our beautiful blue planet write me, and I always write back, I might even mail you some to try out for yourself.
